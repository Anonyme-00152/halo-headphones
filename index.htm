Some sounds need silence around them to be heard.
Listening with intent creates focus, presence, and a deeper connection with music. Halo is built around that simple truth.

Who it's for:

This is made for people who truly listen. It

keeps the world quiet and the sound honest, so you can stay inside your music or your work without noise in the way.

Everything adapts through the app, where

your sound is tuned to your ears and adjusts on its own to every place you go.

Commuters & Travelers

Trains, planes, and busy streets fade into the background. Adaptive noise cancelling reacts to every change around you, so a long commute becomes quiet time — for music, podcasts, or simply a moment to think.

Musicians, Creators & Producers

Detail matters when you make sound. Tuned drivers and a neutral profile let you hear mixes as they are, while low-latency mode keeps audio and video in sync. Everything you hear is honest, precise, and ready to shape into something bigger — without breaking the flow.

Remote Workers & Teams

Meetings start anywhere and stay clear. Eight microphones isolate your voice from cafés and keyboards, while multipoint switches between laptop and phone on its own — keeping your attention in the conversation, not on the noise.

Your sound profile is synced
to every device you own
Your settings. Already there.
Your personal EQ and hearing profile follow you from phone to laptop to tablet. Switch devices mid-song without pairing again, keep your presets in one place, and pick up where you left off. Your listening becomes part of a system that is personal, consistent, and always ready.
No hiss. No pressure.
No random dropouts.
AI-powered sound
Audio is processed in real time and refined quietly in the background. AI recognizes voices, music, and places to balance noise cancelling, sharpen dialogue, and protect your hearing. The technology stays invisible, so your focus stays on sound.

Works like a system.
For you, it’s just quiet
You press play and everything else fades. No settings to hunt for, no modes to switch between. The cushions seal softly, the sound stays clean, and nothing pulls you out of the moment. Nothing changes in how you listen — only how deep you can go.
